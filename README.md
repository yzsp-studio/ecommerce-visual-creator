## 🇨🇳 中文

把原始商品资料转成可直接交给设计师或生图模型执行的视觉方案，产出可用的电商主图、详情图，并在用户最终确认后交付 Photoshop 多图层源文件。坚持证据优先：用户提供的包装、参数、资质、检测、品牌资料优先于已核验的官方最新平台规则，再然后是参考资料与合理假设。缺资料时标记「待确认」，不补造销量、评价、证书、检测、专利、资质、功效、排名或平台规则。同一套图先锁定产品身份、构图、色彩、光影、字体、背景与禁止漂移项，再写细节；涉及尺寸、禁限词、类目政策与审核规则时优先查官方最新来源，无法核验就明确标注。按意图自动路由到标准模式、一次性交付模式、审查/改稿模式或设计师交接模式，也可按需制作商品宣传视频。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 商品事实 + 参考图 → 可执行的电商主图、详情图，并在最终确认后交付 Photoshop 多图层源文件与成品预览
- 先出《视觉策略与证据账本》锁产品身份、构图、色彩、光影、字体、背景与禁止漂移项，再写文案与生图 Prompt
- 证据分级：用户资料 > 已核验官方最新平台规则 > 参考文件 > 合理假设；缺资料标「待确认」，不补造销量、评价、证书、专利、资质、功效、排名
- 尺寸、禁限词、类目政策、主图要求与审核规则优先查官方最新来源，无法核验时标注「未核验」，不凭旧规则下结论
- 视频生成前必须在标准候选中显式选定生成模型，并核验所选模型的时长、参考容量、画幅与分辨率，不沿用旧设置
- 按意图自动路由标准模式、一次性交付模式、审查/改稿模式、设计师交接模式，只交付你需要的那一层级，不为流程完整重复追问

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns raw product material into a visual plan a designer or image model can execute directly, producing usable e-commerce main images and detail pages, then delivering layered Photoshop source files once you finally confirm the version. It is evidence-first: packaging, specs, qualifications, test reports and brand material you provide outrank verified official platform rules, which outrank reference material and reasonable assumptions. Missing information is marked "to confirm" — it never invents sales, reviews, certificates, tests, patents, qualifications, efficacy, rankings or platform rules. Each image set locks product identity, composition, colour, lighting, typography, background and no-drift items before fine detail is written. Size, prohibited terms, category policy and review rules are checked against the latest official sources, and marked unverified when they cannot be confirmed. Four execution modes route automatically: standard, one-shot delivery, review/revise, and designer handoff; promotional product video can be produced on request.

### ✨ What it does

- Product facts + reference images → executable main images and detail pages, with layered Photoshop source files and previews after final confirmation
- First outputs a Visual Strategy & Evidence Ledger locking product identity, composition, colour, lighting, typography, background and no-drift items before copy or generation prompts
- Evidence hierarchy: your material > verified latest official platform rules > reference files > reasonable assumptions; gaps are marked "to confirm" with no invented sales, reviews, certificates, patents, qualifications, efficacy or rankings
- Sizes, prohibited terms, category policy, main-image requirements and review rules are checked against the latest official sources and flagged unverified when they cannot be confirmed
- Before any video generation, the generation model must be explicitly chosen from the standard candidates, with duration, reference capacity, aspect ratio and resolution verified rather than carried over from an old task
- Routes automatically across standard, one-shot delivery, review/revise and designer-handoff modes, delivering only the layer you asked for instead of re-asking known facts
